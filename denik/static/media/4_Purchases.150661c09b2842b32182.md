
# Purchases

You can expand your project any time with extending the user limit or cloud limit. Payment service is provided by the [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) service. For more detail about payment terms & conditions please follow to Stripe site.

## Extend cloud space limit

When you reach your cloud space limit you can extend it by:
1. Go to `Purchases` list in the left navigation bar.
2. Tap on the button under progress bar which says the value you would like to extend with your current limit.
3. Fill in the payment form with your payment card number.
4. Confirm by tapping on the `Pay now` button.

## Extend user space limit

When you reach your user space limit you can extend it by:
1. Go to `Purchases` list in the left navigation bar.
2. Tap on the button under progress bar which says the value you would like to extend with your current limit.
3. Fill in the payment form with your payment card number.
4. Confirm by tapping on the `Pay now` button.

## Subscribe to the subscription plan

By default each project is set to the free plan. This mean you will not have to pay anything for the usage.
In case you would need some advanced features which offers only higher subscription plans, you can subscribe for the plan with following:
1. Go to `Purchases` list in the left navigation bar.
2. Tap on the `Choose plan` under progress bar.
3. Choose the plan from the dropdown menu.
4. Fill in the payment form with your payment card number.
5. Select monthly or annual payment.
6. Confirm by tapping on the `Pay now` button.

## Refund purchase

You can refund with these steps:
1. Go to `Purchases` list in the left navigation bar.
2. Choose the purchase you want to refund.
3. Click on `Refund` button.

Note: Purchases are refundable 14 days after the purchase date.

## Change subscription

You can change subscription with these steps:
1. Go to `Purchases` list in the left navigation bar.
2. Tap on the `Choose plan` under progress bar.
3. Choose the plan from the dropdown menu.
4. Fill in the payment form with your payment card number.
5. Select monthly or annual payment.
6. Confirm by tapping on the `Pay now` button and your plan will be changed.
