
# Records

Records simulate daily records and updates from the process. Feel free to use them also in terms of informing about accidents and announcements.

## Creation of the record

You can create record by following of these steps:
1. Go to the `Records` list by clicking in the left navigation bar.
2. Click on `Create record` button.
3. Wait for a while until the weather information are gathered for the location of the project.
4. Fill in the the record content.
5. Select the record category if needed.
5. Upload record attachments by clicking on the `Upload Files`. You can delete it by clicking on the three dot button.
6. Click on the `Save` button in the right corner.

Note: It may happen that this operation is not available for you due to your role on the project. If that happens please contact your project creator.

## Detail of the record

To see the record detail you may follow these steps:
1. Go to the `Records` list by clicking in the left navigation bar.
2. Click on `Details` or `Edit` button.
3. Wait for a while until the information get loaded.
4. If needed you can download the record attachment by clicking on the three dot button in the attachments list.

Note: It may happen that this operation is not available for you due to your role on the project. If that happens please contact your project creator.

## Deletion of the record

Record can be deleted until it is not signed. Follow these steps:
1. Go to the `Records` list by clicking in the left navigation bar.
2. Click on the red button `Delete`.

Note: It may happen that this operation is not available for you due to your role on the project. If that happens please contact your project creator.

## Making record signed

With signing of the record you confirm its validity and encrypt its content. Signatures are unique feature which prevent manipulation with a data.
For signing of the record follow these steps:
1. Go to the `Records` list by clicking in the left navigation bar.
2. Click on `Details` or `Edit` button.
3. Click on `Sign` button.
4. Fill in verification field with the SMS code you received.
5. You can download signatures in the `Signatures` section in the record detail.

Note: It may happen that this operation is not available for you due to your role on the project. If that happens please contact your project creator.
